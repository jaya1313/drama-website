About Shark Tank India

India's leading startup investment reality show

What is Shark Tank India?

Shar Tank India is a business reality television series where aspiring entrepreneurs pitch their startups to a panel of investors known as "Sharks".The Sharks evaluate the business ideas and decide whether to invest their own money in exchange for equity.

How the Show Works

  • Entrepreneurs present their business idea
  • Sharks ask questions and evaluate the pitch
  • Negotiations happen for equity and valuation
  • Deals are either accepted or rejected

Impact on Indian Startups

The Show has helped bring startup culture into Indian Households,inspiring young entrepreneurs and providing visibility,funding,and mentorship to promising ideas.
